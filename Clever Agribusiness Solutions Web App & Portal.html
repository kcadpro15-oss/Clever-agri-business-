Agribusiness Project Abstract

Title: Digitizing Smallholder Produce Value Chains in Central Uganda

Submitted By: Clever Kakande

Abstract: This project evaluates the economic viability of integrated web-based agricultural platforms in connecting rural farmers with urban consumers. Through a combination of real-time price indexing, organic soil conditioning, and solar drip irrigation, the proposed agribusiness model reduces post-harvest losses from 35% to under 8%.

Key Research Recommendations:

  • Standardize digital price metrics across regional markets.
  • Expand bio-fertilizer accessibility to smallholder farming cooperatives.
  • Implement cellular soil moisture probe networks.
